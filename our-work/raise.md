RAISE is a developing initiative by The Refract Foundation focused on helping schools, educators and students navigate the growing role of artificial intelligence in education.

As AI becomes increasingly common in classrooms and everyday life, RAISE aims to provide practical guidance that supports safe, ethical and effective use of these technologies.

The programme is built around five core principles which encourage thoughtful and responsible adoption of AI within educational settings.

# Why this matters

Artificial intelligence is already transforming how students learn, research and create.

While these technologies present significant opportunities, they also raise important questions around:

- Academic integrity
- Critical thinking
- Digital literacy
- Privacy and safety
- Responsible use

RAISE aims to support schools and students in understanding both the opportunities and challenges presented by AI.

# What we're developing

We are currently exploring:

- A practical framework for AI use in education
- Guidance for students and educators
- Research into attitudes towards AI
- Resources that promote responsible adoption
- Opportunities for discussion and consultation

All development work will be informed by young people, educators and industry professionals.
