Census is a youth-led research initiative by The Refract Foundation to help schools and workplaces better understand how students and staff use technology in their everyday lives.

Census gathers anonymous data on:

- Digital skills and confidence
- Online safety awareness
- Access to devices and the internet
- The emerging trend of AI

The objective is to identify patterns in digital literacy and highlight where additional investment may be needed within individual institutions and across the region.

# Inside the survey

The survey takes around **5–7 minutes** to complete, with no right or wrong answers. Demographics questions are optional — students can skip anything or answer **"Prefer not to say"**. Sections 2–5 feed directly into the four Digital Literacy indices.

Confidence varies by task &mdash; from 4.71 / 5 for using a smartphone to 3.28 / 5 for sending emails and attachments. Device access is uneven too: just two-thirds of students have a desktop computer outside school, and one in six have no laptop at all. The [full OSCA Census report](/assets/OSCA%20Student%20Census%20Report%202526.pdf) covers access, confidence, online safety and AI in detail.

# Why this matters

Digital skills are now vital for learning, communication and work, however levels of confidence and access vary across the region.

Census turns anonymous, aggregated responses into the evidence schools need to plan their digital strategy.

All responses are anonymous and we report in an aggregated form.

# How does Census work?

  <div class="step">
    <span class="step__num">1</span>

      <h3 class="step__title">School registration</h3>
      <p class="step__desc">Schools express interest in participation and nominate a staff contact.       <p class="step__desc">Refract creates a secure survey link for students to complete during tutor time, IT lessons, or in their own time at home. If the staff survey is opted into, staff access a specific form they can complete in their own time.</p>

    <span class="step__num">3</span>
    <div class="step__body">
      <h3 class="step__title">Data collection</h3>

# What schools receive

Every participating school gets a summary report and access to a data-rich dashboard containing:

- **Key statistics** &mdash; a clear picture of digital access, confidence, safety and development across the school
- **Trends and observations** &mdash; patterns identified from the responses
- **Suggested focus areas** &mdash; where additional investment or support may be needed
- **Visual charts** &mdash; accessible overviews for staff, governors and leadership teams
- **A data dashboard** &mdash; raw and filtered data for deeper exploration

The platform is branded to each school, so students and staff take part in a familiar, trusted environment.

# Privacy

Protecting participants' privacy is a core part of how Census is designed.

Census is designed to minimise the collection of personal information. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, it does not collect names, email addresses, student identifiers or other directly identifying information. Please do not include personal information in any free-text responses unless specifically requested.

Most findings are analysed and reported in aggregated or anonymised form to help identify trends and inform educational improvement, without identifying individual participants.

For full details about how we collect, use and protect information, please read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
