Futures is a developing careers programme by The Refract Foundation designed to help young people discover opportunities within the technology sector.

Technology careers are often discussed in schools, but many students have limited exposure to the people, pathways and experiences that can help them understand what those careers actually involve.

Futures aims to bridge that gap by providing accessible insight into the world of technology and innovation.

# Why this matters

The UK's technology sector continues to grow and offers a wide range of exciting career opportunities.

Many young people would benefit from greater access to:

- Industry role models
- Career guidance
- Professional networks
- Skills development opportunities
- Real-world insight into technology careers

Futures aims to help students make informed decisions about their future education and employment pathways.

# What we're developing

We are currently exploring:

- Career insight events
- Guest speakers from industry
- Employability and skills workshops
- Resources highlighting technology careers
- Opportunities to connect students with professionals

The programme will focus on showcasing realistic and achievable routes into the technology sector.
