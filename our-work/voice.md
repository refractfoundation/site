# What is happening?

The UK government is proposing new rules that could:
- Restrict or completely ban social media use for under 16s
- Introduce social media 'curfews' for 16-17 year olds
- Limit features like messaging, livestreaming and content sharing

These changes would reshape how young people across the UK communicate, learn and socialise online.

However, young people seem to be missing from the government's discussions.

# Why we're doing this

Voice exists to:
- Collect real experiences from young people
- Understand what social media means in daily life
- Highlight both risks and benefits of the ban
- Present youth evidence to MPs and policymakers

We believe that young people should be present in making decisions about young people.

# Why this matters

Across the UK and around the world, governments, schools and technology companies are debating how social media should be regulated and what measures are needed to protect young people online.

These discussions frequently involve topics such as:

- Social media age restrictions
- Online safety
- Digital wellbeing
- Platform accountability
- Youth participation in policymaking

While these issues directly affect young people, their perspectives are often underrepresented.

# What we're developing

We are currently exploring:

- Youth consultations and surveys
- Research into young people's views on digital policy
- Opportunities for young people to contribute to public discussions
- Reports and recommendations informed by lived experiences
- Partnerships with schools and community organisations
