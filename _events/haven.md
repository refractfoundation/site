Haven is a teen game jam run by [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com), the global nonprofit network of teen coders, with local events in over 200 cities.

Supported by workshops, lightning talks and mentors, teams built their games around the theme “Bounce Back”. The day finished with presentations and a peer vote, with free food and prizes courtesy of our sponsors.
