On Saturday 14 November, Refract Foundation will host Haven West Midlands at the University of Wolverhampton Science Park: a free game jam for teenagers aged 13 to 18.

It will be our second Hack Club event, following Campfire Birmingham in February.

## What is Haven?

[Haven](https://haven.hackclub.com/west-midlands) is a game jam run by [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com), the global nonprofit network of teen coders, with local events in more than 200 cities. Attendees spend the day building a video game from scratch, supported by workshops, lightning talks and mentors, with free food and prizes.

## The day

- **08:30** Doors open
- **09:00** Opening ceremony and workshops
- **12:30** Lunch
- **14:00** Lightning talks
- **17:00** Dinner
- **17:45** Submission deadline, presentations and voting
- **19:00** Venue closes

## Who can come?

Anyone aged 13 to 18. You do not need any experience, and beginners are very welcome. Come on your own and you will be matched with teammates.

The event is free. Sign up on the [Haven West Midlands page](https://haven.hackclub.com/west-midlands), where there is also a Slack channel for questions.

## How else can you help?

If you would like to volunteer or mentor on the day, or to support future events as a sponsor or venue, [get in touch](/contact).
