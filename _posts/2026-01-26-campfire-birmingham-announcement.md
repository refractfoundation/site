On Saturday 28 February, Refract Foundation will host Campfire Birmingham at Ormiston Sandwell Community Academy: a 12-hour game jam for teenagers aged 13 to 18 from across the West Midlands.

It will be our first hackathon, and the first event we have run in partnership with [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com), the global nonprofit network of teen coders.

## What is Campfire?

[Campfire](https://campfire.hackclub.com/birmingham) is Hack Club's annual game jam, where teenagers build games and compete for prizes. It runs in more than 200 cities at once, and ours is one of the local events, known as satellite events, that take part.

Over the day, attendees will build a game from scratch, working alone or in teams.

## Who can come?

Anyone aged 13 to 18 who lives in the region. You do not need any coding experience. If you have never written a line of code, a game jam is a good place to start, and there will be workshops and mentors on hand to help.

## How to get involved

- **Attend:** find out more and sign up on the [Campfire Birmingham page](https://campfire.hackclub.com/birmingham)
- **Volunteer or mentor:** we would love to hear from you, so [get in touch](/contact)
- **Sponsor or support us:** the same applies, [get in touch](/contact)

Our thanks to Ormiston Sandwell Community Academy for providing the venue, and to Hack Club for creating Campfire.
